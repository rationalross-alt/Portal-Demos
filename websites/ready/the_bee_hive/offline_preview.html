Rooted in Craft

More than a coffee shop. A gathering place for the curious.

Founded with a passion for exceptional brewing and local artistry, The Bean Hive was built as a sanctuary from the everyday rush. Every bean we grind is ethically sourced and expertly roasted to bring out rich, complex flavour notes.

Alongside our coffee bar, explore our artisan boutique featuring hand-crafted goods, pottery, and gifts from talented makers right here in Essex.
