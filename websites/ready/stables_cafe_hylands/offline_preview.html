Historic Setting

A Restful Haven After Your Estate Walk

Located in the beautifully converted 18th-century stable block at Hylands Park, we provide the ultimate spot to unwind after exploring 574 acres of historic parkland and ancient woods.

Whether you've just walked the trails with your dog or cycled through the estate, our courtyard welcomes everyone with warm hospitality, muddy boots friendliness, and exceptional coffee.
